## Rules for Future Tasks
When updating or modifying this repository, AI agents must adhere to the following rules:

1. **Do not invent information:** Never fabricate experience, clients, metrics, capabilities, or project results. All claims must be factual and based on existing repository data.
2. **Verify all links and references:** Ensure all markdown links, image references (SVG/GIF), and external URLs are valid and functional before committing changes.
3. **Keep it concise and scannable:** Recruiters and clients skim. Use bullet points, short paragraphs, clear headers, and strong visual hierarchy. Aim for comprehension within 30 seconds.
4. **Maintain professional tone:** The tone should be confident, practical, and engineering-focused (e.g., emphasizing reliability, testing, and business value over hype or fragile demos).
5. **Focus on the goal:** Every addition or modification should increase the likelihood of securing freelance contracts or remote job interviews in the target roles.
